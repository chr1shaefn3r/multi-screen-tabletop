Tiletop

Put phones and tablets side by side on a table and turn them into one big game board.

I'm the game master

  1. The game master opens a table and gets an 8-digit code.
  2. Everyone else opens this page and joins with the code. Use the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Lay the devices next to each other and swipe in one quick line from the game master's screen across onto the next screens. They line up by themselves.
  4. Play chess or Mensch ärgere Dich nicht on the big board. To move a chess piece to another screen, tap it and then tap the target square.

Tip: turn off auto-rotate and use fullscreen. On iPhone, use Share → "Add to Home Screen".
